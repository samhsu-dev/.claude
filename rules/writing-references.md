# Writing Standards — Sources

Bibliographic backing for the authorities cited in `writing.md`. Every entry has a free, openly accessible URL. Sources without one are not listed: an unfetchable reference grounds nothing an agent can check.

---

## Style and Clarity

- **Strunk & White, *The Elements of Style*** (4th ed., Longman, 1999; Strunk's 1918 text public domain).
  Grounds: "omit needless words"; one idea per sentence.
  URL (1918 ed.): https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/37134

## Scientific Writing

- **Gopen & Swan, "The Science of Scientific Writing"** (*American Scientist* 78(6):550–558, 1990).
  Grounds: stress position (new information at sentence end); reader-expectation structure.
  URL: https://www.americanscientist.org/article/the-science-of-scientific-writing

- **Peyton Jones, "How to Write a Great Research Paper"** (Simon Peyton Jones, Microsoft Research).
  Grounds: lead with a concrete example then generalize; inline forward references; cut the canned roadmap.
  URL: https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/academic-program/write-great-research-paper/

- **Knuth, Larrabee & Roberts, *Mathematical Writing*** (MAA Notes 14, 1989; Stanford CS report).
  Grounds: define a term at first dependence; concrete referent over abstract label.
  URL (full PDF): https://jmlr.csail.mit.edu/reviewing-papers/knuth_mathematical_writing.pdf

## Metadiscourse and Phrasing

- **Manchester Academic Phrasebank** (John Morley, University of Manchester).
  Grounds: neutral academic register; sanctioned phrasings.
  URL: https://www.phrasebank.manchester.ac.uk/

## Precision and Discipline

- **Dijkstra, EWD manuscripts** (E.W. Dijkstra Archive, University of Texas at Austin).
  Grounds: no self-invented terms; degree claims over absolutes; precision of reference.
  URL: https://www.cs.utexas.edu/~EWD/

- **IEEE / ACM editorial guides** (IEEE Author Center; ACM publication style).
  Grounds: banned vague qualifiers; Latin-abbreviation and register conventions.
  URLs: https://ieeeauthorcenter.ieee.org/ , https://www.acm.org/publications/authors
